Problem:Given an integer array, find the maximum product of two integers in it.
Algorithm: We first sort our original array, then the maximum product will either be the product of the first two elements or the product of the last two element.
Testcases:
-   Input:  A = {2,6,-3,7,1,-8,9,0}
    Output: Pair is (7;9)

-   Input:  A = {-43, 42, 67, 52, 56, -99}
    Output: Pair is (-99;-43)
